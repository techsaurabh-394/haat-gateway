title: lexicon: understand 'sawa' (1.25) and 'paune' (0.75)
labels: lexicon, good first issue
---
People say *sawa kilo* (1¼ kg) and *paune do kilo* (1¾ kg). `numbers` in the lexicon handles single words like *dedh* (1.5).

**Do:** support *sawa* (1.25) and *paune* followed by a number (that number minus 0.25) in `Lexicon.parseSegment` in `src/lexicon/normalize.ts`.
**Done when:** tests for `sawa kilo atta` (1250 g) and `paune do kilo chawal` (1750 g) pass.
