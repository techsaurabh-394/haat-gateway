title: lexicon: 'pav' means bread and also a quarter kilo
labels: lexicon, help wanted
---
In Mumbai *pav* is bread, but *pav kilo* (or *paav*) means 250 g. Today *pav* is only a bread variant.

**Do:** when *pav* is followed by *kilo*, read it as 0.25 kg; otherwise keep it as bread.
**Done when:** `pav kilo paneer` gives paneer at 250 g, `2 pav` gives bread × 2, both tested.
