SSE streaming exists (`GET .../events` with `Accept: text/event-stream`). Spec 8.6 also allows `text-delta` events with pieces of a message before the full `message` event.

**Do:** let the assistant emit deltas through the conversation store's listeners (deltas have no SSE `id` and are never stored).
**Done when:** a test sees `event: text-delta` lines followed by the complete message, and a reconnect never replays deltas.
