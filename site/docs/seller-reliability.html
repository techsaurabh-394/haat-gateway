Seller reliability

An open label, and its one small job.

2 min readEdit on GitHub

Haat keeps a short history of how each seller answered Haat's own calls, and shows it openly. It is a label, not a score, and it has one small job in choosing a seller.

#What is measured

For every direct call to a seller (select, init, confirm, status, cancel) Haat records whether the seller answered, stayed silent (ACKed but sent no callback in time), or rejected it (NACK or a 4xx). Answers that arrive after Haat stopped waiting are counted as late and shown, but they do not erase the silence that came first.

Searches are not counted: they go through the Beckn gateway to many sellers at once, so nobody can say who stayed silent.

The label uses the seller's last 50 calls, so an old bad patch is forgiven and a new one is not. A rejection is left out of the label, because it can be Haat's own bad request.

LabelMeaning
reliableanswered 95% or more of its recent calls
mixed80% to under 95%
unreliableunder 80%
not_enough_datafewer than 10 recent calls; no judgement is made

slow is added when the average answer takes longer than 3 seconds.

#What it does when choosing a seller

It only breaks an exact tie. Sellers are compared by items covered, then items total. Only when two sellers are equal on both does the one that answered Haat more reliably win, and the explanation says so (same_total_more_reliable). An unreliable seller still wins when it covers more or costs less. not_enough_data ranks with mixed, so a new seller is not punished.

#What it does not know

It says only that a seller answers Haat. It cannot see cancellations by the seller, product quality, late deliveries, or what customers think. The "why this seller?" text says exactly this next to the label. Do not read it as a rating.

#Where it shows up

  • GET /api/v1/search: suggested_basket.why.reliability (and alternatives[].reliability).
  • "Why this seller?" text, in English and Hindi, when the label is not not_enough_data.
  • GET /ops/overview: per seller reliability and late, next to the counts.

#Storage

Kept in the store (seller_stats), so a restart with HAAT_DB does not wipe it. With the memory store it is lost on restart. Bounded to 500 sellers. A failing store never breaks an order.
